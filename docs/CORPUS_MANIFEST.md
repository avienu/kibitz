# Corpus manifest

SHA-256 fingerprints of the git-ignored measurement inputs, so a
second desk can verify that a re-run used the same corpora the
published figures were computed on. Regenerated by
`scripts/corpus_manifest.sh`; stale hashes mean the figures and
the corpus have diverged and the most recent measurement wins.
